# How I work

I worked with screens for almost twenty five, thirty years. Any interface, I designed.

I am present where the process, application or the underlying problem is most present.

## The process

I'm a UX researcher. I think the digital developments are developer driven and not organised from the on site, in real life and starting with the actual problem.

1. Go into the field. Real in life experiencing. Seeing the actual product at work, the problems, the customer interaction.
2. Interview. Interviews, to separate the must haves from the nice to haves.
3. Translate. The stakeholder audits the output. I just translate.

Building on assumption is the mother of all fuckups.

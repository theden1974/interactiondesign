How I work

I worked with screens for almost twenty five, thirty years. Any interface, anything that has an interface, I designed. I did software.

What I think is my value proposition is that I am present where the process, application or the underlying problem is most present, or in other cases can recognise the source of its purpose.

I saw my own job becoming less relevant, so I was basically searching for my irrelevance to stay relevant.

The process

I'm a UX researcher and have a human centered mindset. I think the digital developments are developer driven and not organised from the on site, in real life and starting with the actual problem.

  1. Go into the field. Real in life experiencing. Seeing the actual product at work, the problems, the customer interaction.
  2. Interview. Interviews and the Kano model, to separate the must haves from the nice to haves.
  3. Translate. There is always a translation between the stakeholder that thinks what is needed and the actual user that can express or audit what is needed.
  4. The stakeholder judges. The output is audited by the actual stakeholder for whom the problem occurs. I just translate.

Building on assumption is the mother of all fuckups.

The system behind it

I'm building a sovereign second brain, built as a recording studio of my expertise from the past 30 years. I'm building it to have a better understanding about what makes me irrelevant.

RecordConversations, talks, field notes, expert interviews. Kept as they were said.
VerifyRaw first. Nothing counts unless the exact words are in the source. Don't trust, verify.
RecogniseWhen a conclusion is repeated from multiple sources, not only from myself, it becomes something you can build on. A local model works in the background on every new recording.
PerformOutput for a venue: these sites, a talk. I read documents of clients through my lens and the output documents are shared back to the clients.

Knowledge is not always truth. It needs to be validated and calibrated with every project that uses AI. I noticed the lock-in of the different models and see the direction it is heading. The reasoning is rented. The recordings stay on my own machine.

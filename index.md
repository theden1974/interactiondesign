## Observations

### 1. DO YOU TRUST AI AS MUCH AS YOUR LAWYER?

Architect used AI to summarise a legal document. Cited specific paragraphs. The paragraphs were fabricated. Sounded legitimate. You cannot tell AI to be an expert in something it is not.

Source: Legal, Architecture

### 2. THE TEACHER LET AI BUILD THE EXAM. EVERY STUDENT FAILED.

Students scored 2s and 3s. They knew the material. Teacher looked again manually. Lowest grade became a 6. The expertise was there. He chose not to use it.

Source: Education

### 3. THE FIX WAS PUBLISHED IN 1937. THE SMOOTHIE INDUSTRY MISSED IT.

Spinach has 10x less iron than published. Decimal error from the 1870s. Corrected. Never traveled. Now it is in the training data.

Source: Nutrition, Data

### 4. BEST IN THE WORLD. $52 IN 43 MINUTES.

Joshua Bell. Famous violin player. Earned a couple of dollars performing in a Washington DC metro station. Same talent. Same Stradivarius. Sells out concert halls at $100 a ticket.

Source: Music

### 5. THE WHOLE DEPARTMENT. USELESS DURING THE FLOOD.

Every permit registered. Every format defined. Water rises. No protocol. The starting point was never floods. It was administration. That is the starting point that went bankrupt.

Source: Government, Waterschappen Hollands Delta

### 6. THE BACTERIAL CAUSE WAS PROVEN IN 1984. PHARMA KEPT SELLING ANTACIDS.

H. pylori bacteria cause a major share of stomach ulcers. Curable with a two-week antibiotic course. Proven in 1984. Pharma kept building the antacid category for years. The correction was published. It just didn't travel fast enough.

Source: Medicine, Pharmaceutical industry

### 7. THE PLATFORM OWNS THE INFRASTRUCTURE. THE PLATFORM OWNS YOUR WORK.

Your CRM holds your customer relationships. Your project tool holds your team's workflow. Your design tool owns your file format. The platform changes the terms. Your business depends on infrastructure you don't control.

Source: Knowledge work, Platform sovereignty

### 8. THEY ALREADY DECIDED THEY NEEDED AN APP.

The funding was to help long-term unemployed people work in the care of the elderly. I asked to interview the elderly still living at home. They were not good at mobiles and apps. They wanted to be self sufficient. My concept: no app for the elderly, only an app for the employee working in the flat. It was not built out.

Source: Elderly care, field research

### 9. HOW DO YOU KNOW IF YOUR AI MODELS ARE BASED ON THE RIGHT DATA?

The task was optimizing app screens for dietitians. It was not so much the dietitian that needed the right app, but making the patient the center. A patient weight over time has so many input points. Doctor, at home, nurse. Morning, afternoon.

Source: Healthcare, field research

### 10. 2.5 FASTER CPU. NORMAL PEOPLE WON'T SEE IT.

New laptops: 2.5 faster CPU, GPU data speeds, X amount faster than the previous generation. Normal people won't see measurable changes in their use. Intelligence and reasoning are purposeless in my opinion.

Source: Technology, AI

### 11. SOMETHING GOES WRONG. AI CONFIRMS IT WAS EXACTLY THE INTENTION.

The output is always reasonable or even confirming. Something goes wrong and it confirms how nice it is that this was recognised, that this was exactly the intention. It needs to be visible that a mistake was made, or that there is no complete information to give an answer at all.

Source: AI output, government conversation, translated from Dutch

### 12. AI CAN'T TELL TIME FROM A CLOCK WITHOUT NUMBERS.

The source AI learns from is created by images that have the aesthetic time, because that looks the best. This reminds me of the way AI learns how to recognise truth if the only truth is one sided.

Source: AI training data, X post

### 13. AI IS NOW LIKE THE DEVELOPER AND THE MANAGER.

Without the UX researcher, the UX lead and the service designer that know what problem needs to be solved. My guess: AI can manufacture reasonable output for somebody without the field knowledge, who figures it good enough. And the senior with years of experience catches the faults.

Source: AI, product development

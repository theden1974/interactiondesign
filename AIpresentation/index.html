The journey
DON'T TRUST.
VERIFY.

The starting point was not AI. It was Bitcoin. Don't trust, verify — and nobody can touch the ledger. I ran my own nodes, held my own keys, lost sats in broken hardware. Then I took the same rule off money and put it on everything else the platform was holding for me.

Same move, one layer up each time. Not your keys, not your knowledge.

What I have now — without hallucinations
A RECORDING STUDIO OF MY OWN EXPERTISE.

AI's big fault is that it always compresses. It summarizes, it compresses. I record lossless — like music. Not MP3, not MP1. Every transcript, everything that happens, stays raw on my own SSD. When better reasoning arrives, I run the recording again and it finds new connections. Nothing was thrown away.

The reason it has no hallucinations is not a feature I trust. It's a rule I enforce. Nothing counts as verified unless it carries its source and an exact quote from that source, and the quote still matches the source word-for-word. No source, no marks, no match — it stays labelled as unverified. Knowledge needs proof of fact: prove the data is real from the ground up, not some hallucination.

And the last check is not the machine. Only the expert can audit the output. Ask an architect to build you a nice house from a prompt and everything is wrong. I can't be in your shoes — the expert has to be.
